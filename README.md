# SplitSight
### The hidden science behind instant autofocus.

**Presented by IlmForge**

Have you ever noticed how your phone camera turns a blurry image into a sharp one in an instant?

We did too. So we decided to explore what happens behind the lens.

SplitSight is an interactive educational project that uncovers the technology behind smartphone autofocus, focusing on **Phase-Detection Autofocus (PDAF)**.

## What is SplitSight?

Modern smartphone cameras can focus remarkably quickly, but the process behind that speed often goes unnoticed.

SplitSight makes this invisible technology easier to understand through interactive experiments, visual explanations, and simulations.

## Explore the Experience

- **Image Upload:** Upload your own image and experiment with simulated focus.
- **Optical System:** Explore how a camera lens directs light towards its sensor.
- **Dual Pixel Technology:** Discover how split photodiodes can detect differences between two light signals.
- **Signal Analysis:** Explore signal alignment using cross-correlation.
- **Autofocus Controller:** Experiment with a simplified feedback-control simulation.

## The Technology Behind It

The project explores several concepts:

1. **Optics:** How lenses focus incoming light.
2. **Phase Detection:** How differences between light signals can indicate defocus.
3. **Signal Processing:** How cross-correlation can estimate the displacement between signals.
4. **Feedback Control:** How a controller can adjust a simulated lens position towards a target.

## Scientific Scope

SplitSight is an educational simulation, not a physical recreation of a commercial smartphone camera sensor.

It uses simplified models to demonstrate selected principles of autofocus, signal comparison, and feedback control. Actual smartphone autofocus systems can use more complex sensor architectures, calibration, and hybrid algorithms.

## Our Mission

Technology is everywhere, yet the mechanisms that make it work often remain hidden.

Through SplitSight, we aim to make those mechanisms visible, understandable, and interactive.
